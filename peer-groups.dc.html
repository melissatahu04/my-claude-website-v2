How each term flows

Each week moves through a different topic together, so by the end of the term you've covered the full picture, body changes, long-term health, lifestyle, self-care, relationships and more.

Week 1: getting to know each other and settling in
Weeks 2–3: body changes and symptoms, in depth
Weeks 4–6: long-term health, lifestyle and self-care
Weeks 7–9: relationships, support and reflecting together
Each session blends a short introduction to that week's topic with open conversation, led by you, not a lecture.
