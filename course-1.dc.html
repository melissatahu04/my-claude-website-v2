About Melissa

I'm Melissa. I'm currently completing a Master of Medical Science, where I'm researching women's interactions with menopause health information and services. But before any of that, I was a woman diagnosed with early menopause at 41, with more symptoms than I could count and no idea what was happening to me.

That experience, plus my research and the women I've met through the peer groups I facilitate, is what this course is built on. Real understanding, not just theory.
